# v8.5.9 server update

This incremental package is installed over v8.5.8.

Every lab owner can now submit a backup as a template. A template submitted by
a non-Administrator remains private and **Pending approval** until an
Administrator approves it from the dashboard. Approval publishes it to every
portal user. Administrators may remove a pending template instead.

Copy the update files to `/opt/gns3-lab-portal`, restart the service, and
hard-refresh the browser.
