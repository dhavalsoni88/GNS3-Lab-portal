Self-signed certificate

Quick and free, but browsers show a warning until you trust it. Good for a lab, or as a first step. It is installed straight away.

Certificate from a certificate authority (CSR)

1. Create a signing request. The private key is made here and never leaves the server. 2. Send the request to your CA (a public one, or your company's). 3. Paste or upload the certificate it returns, together with any intermediate certificates.
